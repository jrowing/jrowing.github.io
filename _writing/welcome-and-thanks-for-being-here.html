I’m a physics teacher, school leader, and education wanderer who has spent the last few years helping to build an outstanding sixth form, coaching teachers, experimenting, and trying to make physics teaching just a little more joyful and a little more evidence-based.

Recently I packed up shop and headed to the Seychelles to teach in a community school, train staff, and continue my mission to bring high-quality physics education to new contexts. Along the way, I’m writing a book intended to provide all you need as a successful physics teacher, packed with demonstrations, datalogger ideas, and classroom-tested explanations.

This Substack will be where I share:

  • Reflections from the classroom (wherever in the world that classroom happens to be)

  • Physics teaching ideas, resources, and demonstrations

  • Notes from my travels and the schools I work with

  • Insights from my time as an IoP Physics coach

  • Occasional deep dives into tech tools, simulations, or the odd Geiger counter

  • Random geekery that I find interesting (see map image)

  • And the things that go wrong, as well as the things that go right

If you teach science, physics, love physics, or just enjoy peeking inside other people’s experiments—literal or metaphorical—I hope you’ll find something here that sparks your curiosity.
