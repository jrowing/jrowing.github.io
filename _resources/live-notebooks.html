Live notebooks

Python models that run in your browser. Change a number, press Run, and watch the graph respond.

No install and no account. Python starts up in the page, so the first load takes 10–20 seconds; after that it is quick. Your edits stay in your own browser and are not saved to the site.

Mechanics Jumping from space Model Felix Baumgartner's jump from the stratosphere: drag that changes with air density, terminal velocity, and the effect of uncertainty in his mass. Mechanics The Magnus effect Why a spinning ball curves. Numerically solve the 3D flight of a ball with drag and spin, then change the spin axis. Mechanics Rocket take-off From a full-size rocket through the atmosphere to a one-litre water rocket: thrust, changing mass, air density and drag. Astrophysics A satellite round the Sun Model a satellite's path under the combined gravity of the Sun, Earth and Moon. Astrophysics Black-body curves Planck's law, Wien's displacement law and the Stefan–Boltzmann law for stars of different temperatures.
