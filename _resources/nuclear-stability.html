Nuclear stability diagram

Each dot is one nuclide, plotted by its protons (Z) and neutrons (N) and coloured by how it decays.

Stable nuclei sit along a narrow band, the valley of stability. Light nuclei lie close to N = Z. Heavier ones need extra neutrons to offset the repulsion between protons, so the band curves upward. Nuclides with too many neutrons tend to decay by β−, those with too few by β+, and the heaviest by alpha emission.

Hover over a dot to see the nuclide. Click a name in the legend to hide it, or double-click to show only that one. Drag to zoom.
