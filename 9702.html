What's in each topic

Student guided notes

Short, structured notes with the key terms, equations and diagram labels left blank. Students complete them during the lesson, alongside numbered worked examples and questions. Each topic opens with a “By the end of this topic I can…” checklist.

Teacher key

The same pages with every blank filled in and every example and question worked through, so you can mark, model answers, or check your own working.

Print or tablet

Each set of notes comes in two layouts. Use Print for paper copies, or Tablet for students who annotate on an iPad or similar. The content is the same.

Slides

A deck for each topic that follows the notes page by page. Download the PDF to present as it is, or the editable PowerPoint to adapt for your own class.

Suggested use

Teach from the slides while students fill in the student notes, so the lesson has a clear structure and nobody is copying from the board. The teacher key is there to check the blanks and work through the examples and questions.
