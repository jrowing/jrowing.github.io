\lettrine[lines=3,findent=5pt,nindent=-1pt]{G}{o placidly amid the noise and haste}, and remember what peace there may be in silence. \adfast{10} As far as possible without surrender be on good terms with all persons. \adfast{10} Speak your truth quietly and clearly; and listen to others, even the dull and ignorant; they too have their story. \adfast{10}
Avoid loud and aggressive persons, they are vexations to the spirit. \adfast{10} If you compare yourself with others, you may become vain and bitter; for always there will be greater and lesser persons than yourself. \adfast{10} Enjoy your achievements as well as your plans. \adfast{10} Keep interested in your career, however humble; it is a real possession in the changing fortunes of time. \adfast{10} Exercise caution in your business affairs; for the world is full of trickery. \adfast{10} But let this not blind you to what virtue there is; many persons strive for high ideals; and everywhere life is full of heroism. \adfast{10} Be yourself. \adfast{10} Especially, do not feign affection. \adfast{10} Neither be critical about love; for in the face of all aridity and disenchantment it is as perennial as the grass. \adfast{10} Take kindly the counsel of the years, gracefully surrendering the things of youth. \adfast{10} Nurture strength of spirit to shield you in sudden misfortune. \adfast{10} But do not distress yourself with imaginings. \adfast{10} Many fears are born of fatigue and loneliness. \adfast{10} Beyond a wholesome discipline, be gentle with yourself. \adfast{10} You are a child of the universe, no less than the trees and the stars; you have a right to be here. \adfast{10} And whether or not it is clear to you, no doubt the universe is unfolding as it should. \adfast{10} Therefore be at peace with God, whatever you conceive Him to be,
 and whatever your labors and aspirations, in the noisy confusion of life keep peace with your soul. \adfast{10} With all its sham, drudgery and broken dreams, it is still a beautiful world. \adfast{10} Be careful. \adfast{10} Strive to be happy. \hfill
