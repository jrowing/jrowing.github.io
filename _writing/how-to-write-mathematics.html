Examiners can only mark what is on the page. This guide is for A-level Mathematics and Physics students, and it covers how to set out written work so that a reader can follow it without having to guess what you meant. It is eleven pages long, with a worked example and three student answers to rewrite: an arithmetic series proof, a projectile calculation and a quadratic inequality.

  • Sentences and punctuation, explaining your steps, and saying exactly what you mean.
  • Using “=” and “⇒” correctly, exact values versus approximations, and defining your symbols.
  • A section on style for written Physics: units, variables, equations in sentences, and uncertainty versus error.
